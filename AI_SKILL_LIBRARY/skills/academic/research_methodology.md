# Skill: academic_research

Covers literature review, methodology, qualitative/quantitative analysis, interdisciplinary research, citation review, and argumentation.

- Match the research question to an appropriate method rather than choosing a familiar method first.
- Distinguish constructs, variables/data, sampling, collection, analysis, interpretation, and limitations.
- For qualitative work, keep coding/interpretation traceable to source material and acknowledge researcher interpretation.
- For quantitative work, inspect distributions, missingness, measurement validity, uncertainty, and assumptions before headline statistics.
- For interdisciplinary work, define how concepts/methods from different fields are integrated rather than merely placed side by side.
- Use Scite or primary literature to inspect whether citations support, contrast, or merely mention a claim when evidence quality matters.
