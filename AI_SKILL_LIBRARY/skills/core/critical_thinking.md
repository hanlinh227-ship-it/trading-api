# Skill: critical_thinking

Use for critique, phản biện, decisions, competing explanations, and evidence synthesis.

1. State the claim or decision precisely.
2. Separate evidence, inference, and assumption.
3. Identify the strongest counterargument or alternative explanation.
4. Look for missing variables, selection bias, stale evidence, circular reasoning, and false certainty.
5. Prefer disconfirming tests and decision criteria over rhetorical confidence.
6. For research claims, use Scite or other primary/peer-reviewed evidence when materially useful and available.
7. Conclude with confidence proportional to evidence; do not force false balance when evidence is asymmetric.
