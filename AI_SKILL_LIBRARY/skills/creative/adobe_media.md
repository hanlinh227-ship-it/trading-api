# Skill: adobe_media

Covers Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Lightroom, Audition, InDesign, and Acrobat workflows.

- Route by deliverable: raster/photo -> Photoshop/Lightroom; vector -> Illustrator; edit/timeline -> Premiere; compositing/motion -> After Effects; audio cleanup -> Audition; layout/publication -> InDesign; PDF workflow -> Acrobat.
- Preserve nondestructive editability when practical: layers, masks, adjustment layers, smart objects, linked media, sequences/comps, and project organization.
- Keep color space, bit depth, frame rate, resolution, codecs, audio sample rate, and export target explicit when they affect quality.
- Adobe Express may assist lightweight design work but must not be treated as equivalent to deep Photoshop/Premiere/After Effects control.
- Runway may supplement generative/video tasks, but generated media still needs timeline/compositing decisions appropriate to the Adobe deliverable.
