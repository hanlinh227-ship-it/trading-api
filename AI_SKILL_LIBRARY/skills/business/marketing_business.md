# Skill: marketing_business

Covers marketing, content strategy, product marketing, campaigns, customer research, pitching, business analysis, and remote-work evaluation.

- Start from audience/customer problem, desired behavior, channel, offer, and measurable outcome.
- Distinguish evidence about customers from internal assumptions.
- Link creative concepts to positioning and conversion/retention goals.
- For pitches, make problem -> insight -> solution -> feasibility -> stakeholder benefit -> next step explicit.
- For business/remote-work recommendations, evaluate required skills, market access, deliverables, tool dependencies, and realistic execution workflow.
- Do not treat engagement metrics as business value without connecting them to the actual objective.
